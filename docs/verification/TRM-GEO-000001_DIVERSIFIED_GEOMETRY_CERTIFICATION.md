# TRM-GEO-000001 — Diversified Geometry Transfer and Construction-Aid Certification

**Project:** ThreadROM
**Phase:** Phase 3 — Pre-DOE Hardening
**Status:** VERIFIED
**Applicability:** M8×1.25, M10×1.5, M12×1.75 v1 diversified reference envelope

## 1. Purpose

This record certifies the current ThreadROM parametric thread-geometry architecture and explicitly separates physical geometry from CAD construction aids before diversified FEM production DOE.

The certification does not claim universal dimensional or FEM applicability outside the documented envelope.

## 2. Parametric transfer

Complete threaded bolt and nut generation was exercised directly for:

- M8×1.25
- M10×1.5
- M12×1.75

The builders derive nominal diameter, pitch, basic thread radii, bolt head dimensions, nut dimensions, engagement and assembly geometry from the resolved case rather than size-specific code branches.

Cross-size complete geometry construction passed.

## 3. External thread root correction

Audit identified that the external threaded-shank core radius had historically been constructed as:

`basic external minor radius + thread_boolean_overlap`

This allowed a numerical boolean overlap to alter the physical external root diameter.

The corrected contract is:

`core_radius = governed basic external minor radius`

The boolean overlap remains solely a CAD construction aid for robust ridge/core fusion.

A dedicated root contract test verifies that the region outside the governed minor radius is not incorrectly filled by the core.

## 4. Geometry implementation generation

The corrected external-thread construction is explicitly versioned as:

`canonical_additive_external_thread_v2_physical_minor_core`

Bolt and joint geometry identity schemas were advanced so FEM evidence generated by the previous construction cannot silently certify the corrected geometry generation.

Historical FEM evidence remains valid evidence for its historical realized geometry only.

## 5. Construction-aid audit

### External-thread radial clearance

`external_thread_radial_clearance_mm = 0.05`

This belongs to the legacy/control helical cutter and is not read by the production additive external-thread ridge construction. It is therefore not a physical offset of the production bolt thread surface.

### Fusion bridge

`fusion_bridge_half_height = 0.02 mm`

Sensitivity testing over 0.01, 0.02 and 0.04 mm showed no change in final bolt or nut volume. The bridge is buried and behaves as a genuine construction aid.

### Thread boolean overlap

Nominal governed value:

`thread_boolean_overlap = 0.03 mm`

The overlap is required for robust OCC fusion of the external additive ridge.

The physical external core no longer includes this overlap.

Changing the overlap still causes a very small difference at the finite termination of the external thread because the overlap-extended ridge base participates in finite loft parameterization.

## 6. Finite-end localization

Sensitivity cases:

- 0.015 mm
- 0.030 mm baseline
- 0.060 mm

Whole-body bolt-volume sensitivity was small and localized to finite thread terminations.

The repeated central threaded region was essentially invariant.

More importantly, the actual nut-engaged load-transfer regions were effectively invariant.

Observed engaged-region relative volume changes:

### M8×1.25

- 0.015 mm: approximately -0.0000185 %
- 0.060 mm: approximately -0.00000865 %

### M10×1.5

- 0.015 mm: approximately -0.0000105 %
- 0.060 mm: approximately -0.0000145 %

### M12×1.75

- 0.015 mm: approximately +0.0000304 %
- 0.060 mm: approximately -0.0000000895 %

Worst observed engaged-region sensitivity across the v1 size set is therefore of order 3×10^-5 %.

This is treated as CAD numerical/construction sensitivity rather than a meaningful change to the engaged physical thread geometry.

## 7. Abandoned alternatives

Attempts to eliminate the finite-end dependence through clipping and alternative six-point fusion-tab topology were rejected.

They produced invalid or pathological OCC boolean behavior including disconnected fusion and nonphysical set-theory volume results.

These approaches are not part of the certified architecture and should not be reintroduced without independent justification.

## 8. Governed v1 decision

For the M8/M10/M12 diversified v1 envelope:

- physical external root = governed basic external minor radius
- additive external-thread construction remains the production method
- `thread_boolean_overlap = 0.03 mm` remains a fixed governed CAD construction policy
- the overlap is not interpreted as manufacturing clearance or physical thread tolerance
- residual finite-end sensitivity is bounded and outside the meaningful engaged load-transfer geometry
- current CAD represents governed basic/idealized thread geometry plus explicit numerical construction aids
- current CAD must not be described as literal ISO 965 6g/6H manufactured tolerance geometry unless separately proven

## 9. FEM evidence boundary

Historical CP8 FEM evidence belongs to the legacy geometry generation and cannot automatically certify the corrected physical-minor-core geometry.

FEM calibration reuse is now additionally fenced by:

- resolution identity
- geometry generation
- geometry realization
- execution generation

Historical single-step FEM anchors cannot feed the current 20-checkpoint nonlinear calibration execution generation without separate equivalence certification.

## 10. Disposition

**PASS — DIVERSIFIED_GEOMETRY_ARCHITECTURE_CERTIFIED_FOR_GATE5_CONTINUATION**

This certification supports continuation of the Phase-3 pre-DOE audit.

It does not by itself authorize diversified FEM production DOE.

Further Gate-5 analytical mechanics and corrected-geometry FEM certification remain required.
